## What is Ember?

Ember is designed to bring moderation, utility, automation, and community-management tools into a clear Discord workflow. Feature availability may change while the bot is in development.

## Invite Ember

Use the **Invite Ember** button on the website to open Discord's authorisation screen. Choose a server where you have the `Manage Server` permission and review the requested permissions before authorising.

## Required Discord permissions

The exact permissions should follow the commands you enable. A moderation bot commonly needs permissions to view channels, send messages, moderate members, and manage messages. Only grant permissions you understand and require.

## First steps

1. Confirm Ember appears in your server member list.
2. Review its role placement and channel access.
3. Open the planned configuration flow when it becomes available.
4. Test commands in a private staff channel first.
