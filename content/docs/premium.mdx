Ember stays free to use. Two premium options sit on top of that, and both pages are the source of truth for current prices and limits.

## Limit Increase

Raises what Ember can keep up with in a single server. Two plans are available:

- **Max — £2/month:** raised limits across welcome messages, dynamic images, automations, sticky messages, and auto thread messages.
- **Supporter — £4/month:** everything in Max, plus priority support, a supporter role in the Discord, and sneak peeks at new features in the Discord.

The [Limit Increase page](/limit-increase) carries the full Free vs Max/Supporter limit table.

## Custom Branding

Lets Ember match your server's look and feel. Pricing and scope are not confirmed yet, so the [Custom Branding page](/custom-branding) currently shows placeholder content only.
