## What is Ember?

Ember is a planned Discord moderation and utility bot focused on practical community tools.

## How do I invite Ember?

Use the [invite route](/invite), then select a server where you have permission to add apps.

## What permissions does Ember need?

Permissions depend on enabled features. Review Discord's authorisation screen and grant only what your server requires.

## Is Ember free?

A Free tier is planned. Pro and Unlimited prices are placeholders until confirmed.

## How do I configure Ember?

See the planned [configuration guide](/docs/configuration). Confirmed setup steps will be published as the product develops.

## Where can I get support?

Contact the Ember team using the support address in the site footer.
